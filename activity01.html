Activity 1: Drink Coaster

Question

For this activity, the task was to create a coaster design for a fictional character by developing interview questions that would guide the design process. The fictional character my partner and I chose was Satoru Gojo from Jujutsu Kaisen.

Process

We used generative AI to help brainstorm ideas. We then used an AI to pose as this fictional character and used the interview questions to gather information and establish design criteria. Finally, based on the research and insights from the interview, we plugged our question answers into generative AI and generated am image of this new coaster.

Result

The final result was the unique drink coaster design seen in the picture above, featuring aspects of Satoru Gojo from Jujutsu Kaisen, incorporating his signature spells like red, blue, and hollow purple as well as inate abilities like limitless and infinity.
