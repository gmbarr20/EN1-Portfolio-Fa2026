Project 4: Top Spinner

Question

The task here was to use the motors and gears available in the Spike Prime kit to spin a top for as long as possible. There was an emphasis on the specific arrangement of the gears and how different ratios gave different speed outputs. Our challenge was to find which combination of gears and ratios gave the fastest top speed so that our LEGO top would spin as long as possible.

Process

We started by finding which gears would increase the revolution rate for each single turn of the input gears. We found that the large black gears meshed well with the smallest black gears and gave the largest increase in revolutions. We then chained two of those reductions together, as seen in the images, and further chained the reduction to the remaining large gray and small yellow gears. We learned that high torque was needed to turn this long gear chain as fast as possible, so we swapped the medium motor for the stronger large motor and connected the motor and gear assembly to the hub to complete the top spinner.

Result

The result is shown in the images above: the large motor runs the gear train to drive a spinning top that can detach and spin independently. The large motor was able to turn the gears and top at high speed, and the top successfully released to compete for the longest spin.
