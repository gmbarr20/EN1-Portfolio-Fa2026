Project 6: Simple Car

Question

The goal for this project was to get introduced to the motors and vehicle functions of the LEGO Spike Prime system and to create a simple car that could move forward, move backward, and turn in both big and small arcs. An additional challenge was to create the simple car so that it could survive a drop test: the car would be dropped from a height and still be able to perform all original functions.

Process

I began by building the simple car frame with the two medium motors mounted underneath the hub block. I then added a roller ball as the third omnidirectional wheel, providing three points of contact for stability. With the original motor placements and some AI-generated code in the Spike Prime coding environment, the simple car was technically complete. I then tackled the secondary challenge and worked to make the car faster. I added equal gear ratios to both motors so the wheels would turn faster with each motor revolution, then began reinforcing the entire car so it could survive the drop test. I first dropped the initial design from table height and examined where pieces broke or separated. I used frame and joinery pieces to support those areas, then tested again. I repeated this process until the resulting car could consistently survive the drop and drive away.

Result

The result is the simple car shown in the images and video above. The car has mirrored gear reductions on both sides to increase wheel speed, along with brackets and axles running along its length and width for rigidity and structure. The video shows that the simple car can survive a drop from table height and drive away.
